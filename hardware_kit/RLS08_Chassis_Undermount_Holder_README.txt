RLS08 chassis under-mount holder
================================

Designed for the SmartElex RLS08 board shown in the supplied photos.
The pocket accepts either the 120x26 mm manual dimension or the 126x26 mm
retail dimension. The PCB sits in the side ledges; the central underside is
open for the eight IR sensors.

Print one holder. Attach the top frame to the underside/front of the old
250x196x5.4 mm chassis using four M4 screws and washers through the 18x5.2 mm
adjustment slots. Slide the holder sideways/fore-aft before tightening.
Install the board with the sensor/IR side facing the floor. Keep the sensor
face approximately 3-5 mm above the floor; verify this with the wheel and
chassis installed before final tightening.

The PCB size and hole pattern vary across RLS08 listings, so this is a
clearance/retaining frame rather than a hard screw-through-PCB clamp. Use a
small cable tie or two drops of removable adhesive only if the PCB rattles.

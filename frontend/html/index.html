THE STORY

About RideHub

A simpler way to discover local rides.

RideHub is a major project developed as part of academic work at Graphic Era (Deemed to be University), Dehradun. It explores how renters can discover nearby rental shops, compare vehicle listings and request a reservation through one clear experience.

Built around local discoveryShop profiles · vehicle listings · date-based availability · reservation workflows

RideHub is an academic software project and is not an official University-operated rental service. Listings and availability depend on participating shops.

PLEASE READ

Terms of Use

Clear expectations for using RideHub.

  1. Platform role. RideHub helps users discover shops and vehicle listings. The rental agreement is between the customer and the shopkeeper.
  2. Accurate information. Users and shopkeepers are responsible for keeping account, listing and contact details accurate and lawful.
  3. Availability and pricing. Search results are indicative until the shop confirms the requested dates and final terms. Review the displayed price, deposit, documents and cancellation conditions before confirming.
  4. Safe use. Do not misuse accounts, submit false documents, bypass eligibility requirements or use a vehicle unlawfully. Shops may set reasonable rental and handover requirements.
  5. Project status. RideHub is an academic project; features may change during development. This summary is informational and is not a substitute for a signed rental agreement or legal advice.

YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Use only the information needed to run the experience.

  • Account and booking details may be used to sign you in, verify your email and support reservation workflows.
  • Location is used for nearby discovery only when you enter it or grant browser location permission. You can search by area instead.
  • Preferences such as your selected display theme may be saved in your browser.
  • Sharing and safety — do not send passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar numbers, payment credentials or identity-document images to the support email.
  • Academic project notice. This project notice describes intended use, not a guarantee of production-grade retention or security controls. Avoid entering sensitive information unless the relevant feature explicitly requires it.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a vehicle?

Choose a pickup area and dates, select a vehicle category, then compare available local listings and shop details.

Are listings available everywhere?

Listings appear where participating, approved shops have added vehicles. Availability can vary by location and date.

Who confirms my reservation?

The rental shop confirms the request and coordinates handover details. Check your booking status for updates.

How can a shop join?

Create a shopkeeper account, complete the shop profile and submit vehicle listings. Listings may require review before appearing.

How do I contact RideHub?

Email business.starkindustries@gmail.com. RideHub is based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand.
